Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy explains what data Codebase Time Machine collects, why, and how it's handled — both for visitors to this website and for clients who use our repository analysis service.

Who we are

Codebase Time Machine is operated as a small, independent service. For any privacy question, contact us directly at hello@codebasetimemachine.com — you'll reach a person, not a support queue.

What we collect on this website

  • If you email us (including via the "Book a demo" or "Contact" links), we receive whatever you include in that email — typically your name, email address, and message content.
  • This website itself does not use tracking cookies, analytics scripts, or third-party advertising pixels.

What we collect when you use the audit service

When you engage us for a repository audit, we access only what's needed to run the analysis:

  • Git commit history and metadata from the repository you grant access to — commit authors, dates, file paths, and commit messages.
  • Access credentials (a GitHub App installation token, a scoped personal access token, or collaborator access) that you provide for the duration of the scan only.
  • Contact and billing information you provide directly (company name, email, engagement scope).

We do not access, analyze, or retain your application's runtime data, databases, customer data, or any system outside the Git repository you explicitly grant access to.

Retention

The cloned working copy of your repository — and any access token supplied for the scan — is deleted immediately after the report is generated and delivered. We do not retain a copy of your source code. The generated PDF and Excel report are retained only as long as needed to deliver them to you, plus a reasonable period for support purposes, unless you request earlier deletion.

How we use what we collect

  • To perform the repository analysis you've requested and produce your report.
  • To respond to inquiries sent via email.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including under GDPR, if you're in the EU/EEA), you may have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of personal data we hold about you, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, email hello@codebasetimemachine.com.

Security

Repository scans run in a dedicated, temporary workspace unique to that scan. Access tokens are held in memory only for the duration of the scan and are never written to disk or logged. We are a small, early-stage service — we describe our actual current practices here rather than making broader security claims we haven't yet built out.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site or service after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy or how your data is handled: hello@codebasetimemachine.com
